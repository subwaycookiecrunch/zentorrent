Built without compromises.

Modern streaming engineering packed into a lightweight, standalone Go binary.

🎧

Real-Time Dialogue Booster & Spatial EQ

Web Audio API digital signal processing built right into the player. Intelligently amplifies whispered movie lines while compressing loud explosions. Features 6 studio presets: Vocal Clarity, 7.1 Virtual Surround, and Bass Boost.

⚡

54 Tier-1 DHT Trackers

Automatically injects 54 public UDP, HTTP, and WebSocket BitTorrent trackers on the fly to maximize swarm download speeds and unearth rare seeders.

🍿

Watch Party Synchronization

Watch movies together with synchronized playback, private room PINs, live chat, and floating reaction emojis.

🖼️

Floating Mini-Player (PiP)

Press I to detach the stream into a floating corner mini-player so you can multitask or browse other movies without interrupting playback.

🔒

Zero-Cost Private Tunnel

Run zentorrent watchonline to generate an ephemeral encrypted HTTPS link for your TV, iPad, or phone with zero port forwarding.
